# Privacy Policy — Bible Study

**Effective date:** November 8, 2026

Bible Study is a free Bible reading and study app. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — collect.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Bible Study does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

## What stays on your device

- **Your notes and favorite verses** are saved only on your own device (in the app's local storage). They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are never synced to a server. If you delete the app, your notes and favorites are deleted with it.
- **The Bible text and dictionary** are bundled inside the app, so reading and word lookup work offline without contacting any server.

## Donations

The app is free — free to download, with no paywall and no in-app purchases.
Nothing in the app is locked behind a payment. If you choose to support it,
the "Support the Bible Study" button opens Ko-fi (ko-fi.com/rickdemers) in
your browser. Giving is entirely voluntary and never required. Any payment
is handled by Ko-fi, not by this app — the app itself collects no payment
information. Donations help keep the app free and support charitable giving.

## Links that leave the app

The app contains links to a few outside websites, which open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies:

- **Webster's 1828 Dictionary** (webstersdictionary1828.com) — opened when a tapped word has no bundled 1828 entry.
- **e-Sword** (e-sword.net) — free Bible study software for Windows.
- **Bible Mysteries Podcast** (biblemysteriespodcast.com) — a free podcast website.
- **BibleProject** (YouTube) — the video overviews in the Places tab play from YouTube.
- **Bible games and kids' apps** linked from the Fun tab (e.g. Kingdom Quest Games, Superbook, YouVersion) — each opens its own site or store page.
- **Lighthouse Church** (lighthousefam.com, instagram.com/lighthouse.students) and **Ko-fi** (ko-fi.com/rickdemers) — optional links in the app's support and church cards.

We do not control those sites, and we do not receive any information from them about you.

## Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the "Effective date" above will be updated.

## Contact
